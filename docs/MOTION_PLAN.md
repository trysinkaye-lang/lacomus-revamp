# LACOMUS — Phase 4 Product Film Plan

The Phase 3 site already has scroll-linked cinematic motion using campaign stills. These four short films are the next media upgrade once Higgsfield video credits are active.

## Film 01 — Blue Sapphire Opening

**Source:** Phase 1 Blue Sapphire campaign still  
**Duration:** 5 seconds  
**Format:** 16:9, 1080p target  
**Direction:** Very slow dolly-in toward the bottle. A narrow sapphire highlight travels once across the shoulder and cap. Fine haze moves almost imperceptibly. Reflections in the black stone shift naturally. Product remains physically stable and label remains readable. No morphing, no new objects, no fast motion, no text generated inside the video. End frame should feel slightly closer and darker than the first to support the hero scroll transition.

## Film 02 — Pink Sapphire Atmosphere

**Source:** Phase 2 Pink Sapphire campaign still  
**Duration:** 5 seconds  
**Format:** 16:9  
**Direction:** Slow lateral camera slide with very subtle foreground rose-glass parallax. Soft blush light travels across the bottle edge. The bottle remains rigid and brand geometry is preserved. Atmospheric haze breathes gently. No flowers, people, hands, particles, fabric movement, or fantasy effects.

## Film 03 — Emerald Depth

**Source:** Phase 2 Emerald campaign still  
**Duration:** 5 seconds  
**Format:** 16:9  
**Direction:** Controlled low-angle push-in through a dark architectural setting. Deep green reflections evolve on the stone; a faint warm amber line appears and disappears on the bottle edge. Light smoke crosses only the distant background. Product label and cap remain unchanged. Mature, masculine, minimal.

## Film 04 — Signature Duo Closing

**Source:** Phase 2 Signature Duo still  
**Duration:** 5 seconds  
**Format:** 16:9  
**Direction:** Very slow orbit of only a few degrees around the two bottles. Blue and blush reflections subtly converge on the polished base. Keep both bottles perfectly proportioned, fixed in relation to one another, and fully recognizable. No romance clichés, hearts, flowers, hands, or extra props. Ending should settle into a symmetrical premium packshot suitable for the final CTA.

## Integration

The site architecture intentionally separates **scene layout** from **scene media**, so the stills can later be replaced with muted autoplay `<video>` layers using the same sections and overlays. Mobile should continue using still posters unless network and motion preferences allow video.

## Current generation constraint

A 5-second Seedance 2.5 image-to-video generation currently estimates at **32.5 Higgsfield credits per scene**. Four films would therefore require about **130 credits** before variations or retries.
